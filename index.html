Our Story

How it
all
began.

Collin and Celine met on the natural gas trading floor at ExxonMobil, where Celine was interning and Collin had already been working for a few years.

They waited until Celine’s internship was coming to an end before going on their first date: drinks at Padre’s followed by dinner at Coltivare in the Heights. A few more dates followed, and they made it offical over dinner at BCN. Fittingly, their first international trip together was to Barcelona.

Over the next few years, they built a life around favorite local spots like El Tiempo, Clark’s Oyster Bar, and Southern Yankee Crafthouse, along with plenty of trips to Southern California, New York City, and Miami.

Two and a half years later, Collin brought Celine to Aspen and proposed. What started as a quiet moment for the two of them turned into a celebration with family and friends waiting nearby. They spent the rest of the weekend enjoying good food, good company, and the mountains.

Now, March 6th is right around the corner, and they can't wait to celebrate with everyone who has been part of the journey.

The Ceremony

Annunciation Greek
Orthodox Cathedral

Phil and Renata DeMoes raised their twin daughters, Celine and Danielle, in the Annunciation Greek Orthodox Church. The girls grew up deeply connected to the parish community, and Celine has since introduced Collin to one of her family's favorite annual traditions — the Greek Festival, held each October to celebrate Greek culture, food, and Orthodox life.

The DeMoes' are overjoyed to welcome you to their church and to share this magnificent cathedral and ceremony with the family and friends they love most.

The Reception

La Colombe D'or
Mansion House

The Mansion holds deep significance for both families. Phil and Renata DeMoes, parents of the bride, had one of their very first lunch dates at the Mansion House restaurant with Phil's parents visiting from Alabama. It was a place of meaning for what is now a 25-year marriage — and now, in a beautiful full-circle moment, it becomes the setting for the next chapter of the DeMoes' story.

For the groom, the connection is equally personal. Collin has called the hotel Residences at La Colombe D'or home for the past three years — beginning shortly after he and Celine started dating in August of 2023. Their courtship was woven into the fabric of this neighborhood: evening walks along Montrose, oyster-filled dinners at Clark's or Southern Yankee Crafthouse just around the corner, and pre-dinner cocktails at Bar No. 3, the timeless bar tucked inside the Mansion House itself.

Collin and Celine feel deeply humbled and privileged to share their special day with you at such a uniquely meaningful place.

Things to Do

Explore
Houston.

Houston's Montrose neighborhood is one of the most vibrant, walkable, and eclectic areas in the city. Whether you're here for a long weekend or just a day, there's something worth discovering at every turn.

01
Shopping · Dining · Lifestyle
Montrose Collective
An open-air collection of boutiques, restaurants, and coffee shops anchored by Uchi and a rotating cast of Houston's best local brands. A perfect afternoon stop — particularly on a weekend.
888 Westheimer Rd, Houston, TX 77006
02
Art Museum · Free Admission
The Menil Collection
One of the world's great private art museums, free and open to the public. The campus spans shaded oak lawns and several gallery buildings — including the ethereal Rothko Chapel nearby. A true Houston gem.
1533 Sul Ross St, Houston, TX 77006
03
Art Museum · Museum District
Museum of Fine Arts (MFAH)
One of the largest art museums in the United States, with a permanent collection spanning 6,000 years of history. Plan for at least half a day — and don't miss the rooftop for panoramic Houston views.
1001 Bissonnet St, Houston, TX 77005
04
Gardens · Hermann Park
McGovern Centennial Gardens
A stunning 5-acre garden in Hermann Park featuring a spectacular spiral walkway, seasonal blooms, and tranquil reflecting pools. Ideal for an early morning stroll or a peaceful afternoon escape.
1500 Hermann Dr, Houston, TX 77004
05
Golf · 18 Holes · Public
Hermann Park Golf Course
A classic 18-hole public course tucked inside Hermann Park, surrounded by the city's most beautiful green space. Affordable, accessible, and a Houston institution. Great for a casual round before the festivities.
2155 N MacGregor Way, Houston, TX 77004
06
Golf · PGA Tour Stop · Public
Memorial Park Golf Course
Home of the Houston Open, Memorial Park Golf Course is a meticulously maintained 18-hole championship course set against the backdrop of 1,500 acres of Memorial Park. One of the finest public courses in Texas — book well in advance.
1001 E Memorial Loop Dr, Houston, TX 77007
01
Seafood · Oyster Bar
Clark's Oyster Bar
A gleaming Montrose seafood counter with an impressive daily oyster selection, excellent chowder, and the most underrated burger in the neighborhood. Half-price martinis at happy hour. Collin & Celine's favorites are the Clark's Vesper Martinis and Steak Tartare.
3807 Montrose Blvd, Houston, TX 77006
02
Tex-Mex · Casual
El Tiempo Cantina
A Houston institution. Sizzling fajitas and strong margaritas. Classic Tex-Mex at its best.
322 Westheimer Rd, Houston, TX 77006
03
Gastropub · Casual
Southern Yankee Crafthouse
Beloved Montrose gastropub with arguably the best bar food in Houston. Unique pizzas, wood-fired wings, and craft burgers.
1312 W Alabama St, Houston, TX 77006
04
Steakhouse · Upscale
Drake's Hollywood
Montrose's most glamorous dining room. Dramatic interiors, an exceptional raw bar, and a menu centered on prime cuts and indulgent sides. The coldest martini in Houston.
1100 Westheimer Rd, Houston, TX 77006
05
Texas BBQ · Counter Service
The Pit Room
One of Houston's finest Texas barbecue stops.
1201 Richmond Ave, Houston, TX 77006
06
Gulf Coast · All-Day Dining
Perseid
Chef Aaron Bludorn's Gulf Coast-inspired all-day restaurant at Hotel Saint Augustine. Quiet, refined, and quietly exceptional — the hanger steak and Anne potatoes are Houston's best-kept secret. Reserve ahead.
4110 Loretto Dr, Houston, TX 77006
